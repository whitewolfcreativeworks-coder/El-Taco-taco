# El Taco Taco — Meeting Discovery Addendum 001

Status: Exploratory only. No owner approval, commercial agreement, or implementation authorization.

## Current posture
Wait for the owners to reply and arrange a first meeting. A friendly Spanish reply is already prepared outside this repository. Do not initiate builds or request extensive materials before the meeting.

## Key uncertainty
The existing operation is a well-equipped taco truck. The planned Gateway-area second location may be a physical restaurant, but its format and operating model are unconfirmed. Do not assume both locations will offer delivery or even online ordering.

## First meeting: listen before proposing scope
Learn the owners' goals, existing operations, anticipated second-location setup, pain points, budget preferences, and interest in a website, online pickup ordering, delivery, loyalty, and marketing. Invite any menus, prices, logos, photos, location details, and examples they already have, without making preparation a prerequisite.

## Modular options, not commitments
- Informational website and location/menu presentation.
- Online ordering for pickup from one or more locations.
- Delivery from neither, one, or both locations, subject to owner preference and economics.
- Loyalty and promotions.
- Marketing/social content support.
- Optional operations and driver coordination tools.

## Delivery concept held for later evaluation
An interactive map or delivery-distance estimator could show clear charges before checkout. Possible zones or a sliding distance-based fee should be evaluated against actual driving routes, time, driver pay, order minimums, and operating locations. Delivery address may differ from purchaser's home. No radius, fee, compensation, or routing formula is approved. Customer-facing delivery fees and driver compensation are distinct; the proposed 2% platform revenue arrangement is also separate and remains for a later owner discussion.

## Sequence
1. Wait for owner response.
2. Arrange a first meeting; discuss needs and available materials.
3. If both sides wish to proceed, confirm scope and terms.
4. Only then arrange original food, kitchen, and family photography/video with permission, and plan implementation.
